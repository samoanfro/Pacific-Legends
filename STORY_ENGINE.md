# Pacific Legends Story Engine

## Goal

Generate fresh concepts without repeating the same story.

## Step 1 — Select a Story Pillar

Choose one:

- Warrior
- Woman warrior or chiefess
- Navigator
- Shipwreck
- Love and dignity
- Chief's judgment
- Samoan–Tongan conflict
- Alliance or marriage dynasty
- Missionary encounter
- Trader or whaler
- Family survival
- Fa'afafine family/community story
- Oral tradition or legend
- Ocean rescue
- Rebellion
- Returning exile

## Step 2 — Select the Geographic Setting

Choose one specific setting:

- Samoa
- Tonga
- Hawai'i
- Tahiti
- Aotearoa
- Cook Islands
- Marquesas
- Niue
- Tokelau
- Tuvalu
- Wallis and Futuna

Research the chosen setting before writing.

## Step 3 — Select the Time Window

- 1700–1749
- 1750–1799
- 1800–1849
- 1850–1899
- Earlier historical or legendary period, clearly labeled

## Step 4 — Select the Outsider or Viewpoint

- European captain
- Cabin boy
- Naval officer
- Ship's surgeon
- Missionary
- Trader
- Whaler
- Cartographer
- Naturalist
- Runaway sailor
- Mixed-island traveler
- Polynesian child
- Chief's daughter
- Tongan warrior
- Samoan warrior
- Fa'afafine family member
- Elder navigator

Not every story requires a European viewpoint.

## Step 7 — Select the Reveal

- The quiet person is the leader
- The feared warrior chooses mercy
- The outsider needs the navigator
- A woman controls the strategy
- Rival warriors save each other
- The “weak” family member holds the village together
- The chief already knows about the betrayal
- The canoe outperforms the ship
- The old rumor was true
- The enemy becomes kin
- The sacrifice changes the community
- The person everyone overlooks becomes indispensable

- Earned respect
- Reconciliation
- Return home

- Marriage
- Honored burial
- Mercy

- Shared voyage
- Legacy passed to a child
- Outsider's journal entry
- Historical title explained

## Rotation Rules

Do not repeat the same:

- Island two episodes in a row
- Main conflict two episodes in a row
- Character type more than twice in five episodes
- Ending more than twice in ten episodes
- “European laughs, warrior proves him wrong” pattern more than once in four episodes

## Monthly Content Mix

For every eight long-form episodes:

- 2 warrior/action
- 1 woman-centered
- 1 navigation/ocean
- 1 family/love/dignity
- 1 historical conflict or alliance
- 1 legend/oral tradition
- 1 wildcard, including belonging/community stories

## Concept Output Template

For every generated idea, return:

- Working title
- Setting
- Approximate year
- Story label
- Main characters
- Core value
- Central conflict
- Opening hook
- Midpoint reversal
- Final reveal
- Ending
- Historical basis
- Fictional elements
- Visual selling point
- Thumbnail concept
