# Production Pipeline

## Stage 1 — Idea Approval

Generate five concepts.

Score each from 1–5 for:

- Click potential
- Emotional power
- Visual potential
- Cultural value
- Historical interest
- Originality
- Production difficulty

Select the strongest concept, not simply the most violent one.

## Stage 2 — Research Packet

Create a source packet containing:

- Timeline
- Place names
- Political context
- Clothing
- Housing
- Canoes and ships
- Weapons
- Food
- Social roles
- Names and titles
- Weather and geography
- Known uncertainties

## Stage 3 — Story Draft

Generate:

- One-sentence premise
- Hook
- Four-act outline
- Full narration
- Closing historical note

## Stage 4 — Editorial Review

Check:

- Story is original
- Cultural groups are not blended carelessly
- No superiority claims
- No modern objects
- No repetitive AI language
- The hero has flaws
- The opposing side has believable motives
- The ending is earned
- The first 60 seconds creates curiosity

## Stage 5 — Character Lock

Create a reference sheet for every main character.

Do not generate scene images until the character descriptions are locked.

## Stage 6 — Scene Breakdown

Create 35–55 master visual scenes.

Recommended pacing:

- 5–7 seconds during the hook
- 7–12 seconds during dialogue and setup
- 3–6 seconds during action
- Longer holds only when motion, overlays, or environmental effects are added

## Stage 7 — Image Generation

Generate:

- Character portraits
- Establishing shots
- Dialogue close-ups
- Action shots
- Emotional reaction shots
- Objects and environment inserts
- Final legacy image
- Thumbnail candidates

## Stage 8 — Voice Generation

Use one main narrator.

Narration should sound human, measured, and confident.

Do not use exaggerated “movie trailer” delivery for the full episode.

## Stage 9 — Video Assembly

Required:

- 16:9 timeline
- Slow image motion
- Subtle transitions
- Ocean, wind, fire, canoe, village, and ship ambience
- Music under narration
- Dialogue emphasis where needed
- Captions
- Audio leveling
- Historical disclaimer

## Stage 10 — Quality Control

Watch the full export and confirm:

- No repeated image appears accidentally
- Characters remain recognizable
- No modern artifacts
- No broken hands or weapons in key shots
- Captions match narration
- Names are pronounced consistently
- Music does not overpower the voice
- Thumbnail matches the actual story
- Description identifies the work as fiction, retelling, oral tradition, or legend

## Stage 11 — Publish and Review

Record:

- Impressions
- Click-through rate
- Average view duration
- First 30-second retention
- First 60-second retention
- Returning viewers
- Subscribers gained
- Top traffic source
- Best-performing Short
- Comments mentioning emotion, culture, history, or confusion

Use the results to improve the next episode.
