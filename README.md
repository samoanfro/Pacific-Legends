# Pacific Legends Studio v1

## Purpose

Pacific Legends is a cinematic YouTube storytelling brand focused on original historical fiction inspired by Polynesian history, oral traditions, family values, ocean knowledge, warrior culture, women leaders, chiefs, navigators, and encounters with European ships during the 1700s and 1800s.

The channel must be entertaining first, culturally respectful, emotionally satisfying, and designed for repeatable long-form production.

## Core Promise

The ships came with guns, maps, and assumptions.

The islands answered with courage, navigation, family, strength, sacrifice, wisdom, and honor.

## Primary Output

Each production cycle creates:

1. One 12–18 minute long-form YouTube episode.
2. One finished narration script.
3. A scene-by-scene visual plan.
4. Image prompts for every scene.
5. Character reference prompts.
6. Music and sound-effect cues.
7. A thumbnail brief.
8. Three title options.
9. A YouTube description.
10. Chapters, keywords, hashtags, and pinned comment.
11. Three to five Shorts derived from the episode.
12. A fact-versus-fiction historical note.

## Recommended Release Cadence

### Launch Month
- Publish 1 long-form episode per week.
- Publish 3 Shorts per week.
- Build a four-episode buffer before moving faster.

### After the Workflow Is Stable
- Publish 2 long-form episodes per week.
- Publish 4–5 Shorts per week.
- Publish 1 community poll or story teaser per week.

### Recommended Publishing Days
- Monday: Long-form episode
- Tuesday: Short
- Wednesday: Short or historical note
- Thursday: Long-form episode after the buffer exists
- Friday: Short
- Saturday: Short or character feature
- Sunday: Community poll

Quality matters more than volume. Do not publish daily long-form content.
